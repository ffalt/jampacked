Your music, your server, wherever you go.

JamPacked is an Android music player for JamServe. Connect to your own server to browse, play and take your music library offline. JamPacked is a client - it does not host or scan your music.

FIND YOUR MUSIC
Browse artists, albums, folders, genres, series, playlists and podcasts. Search your library, jump through long lists with the A-to-Z index, or explore favourites, recent, frequent and top-rated selections.

PLAY YOUR WAY
Play tracks or build a queue from your library. Shuffle, clear or edit the queue, and keep playback close with the mini player. Open the full player for album artwork, lyrics and a waveform you can scrub through.

MAKE YOUR LIBRARY YOURS
Favourite and rate albums, artists and tracks, and save bookmarks. Listening activity is scrobbled to your server, keeping your history with your own library.

TAKE MUSIC OFFLINE
Pin albums, folders, series or individual tracks for download. Pinned music is available to play without a network connection. Track active downloads and pause them from the downloads screen.

CHOOSE YOUR LOOK
Use the system theme, light, dark or true black for OLED screens.

REQUIREMENTS
A running JamServe server and an account on it are required. JamPacked connects to your server and does not include or provide music.

No ads or subscription. Your library stays on your server.
